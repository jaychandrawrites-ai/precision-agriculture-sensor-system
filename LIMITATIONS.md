# Current limits

- The Yuma and Maricopa comparisons support conclusions for their tested
  periods. The seasonal neural blend gave small differences against daily
  persistence, and simple harmonic regression was competitive. Additional
  untouched stations and years are needed before recommending a model.
- AZMET supplies historical weather. Simulated leaf wetness, soil-water ET
  and modeled cotton stress require independently measured field outcomes
  for physical validation. Nighttime cloud and wind-height inputs to the
  optional ET comparison can be assumptions.
- The native AquaMap firmware/protocol and live sensor calibration remain
  unavailable. Current tests exercise verified archive formats and
  synthetic protocol fixtures.
- No real paired field labels were available for joint temporal-spatial
  training or predictive spatial calibration. Adaptive spatial refinement
  and propagation of full prior parameter uncertainty remain follow-up work.
- The dashboard has loopback integration coverage. Browser rendering and
  live serial UI were not verified. CUDA parity awaits suitable hardware.
